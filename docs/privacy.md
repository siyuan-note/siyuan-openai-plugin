# SiYuan for ChatGPT and Codex Privacy Policy

This policy covers version 0.2.0 of the SiYuan desktop plugin provided by Yunnan Liandi Technology Co., Ltd. The plugin uses the SiYuan command-line program on your computer to work with the workspace you select in a local ChatGPT or Codex conversation.

[Privacy policy for version 0.1.1](https://github.com/siyuan-note/siyuan-openai-plugin/blob/181f8db4465a2c9bc59749e719a95e3f1f670090/docs/privacy.md) · [SiYuan Privacy Policy](https://b3log.org/siyuan/en/privacy.html)

## Data used by the plugin

- **Workspace information.** The selected workspace path is used in local command arguments and may appear in the conversation. The command runs with the local file access and execution permissions available to the conversation.
- **Conversation and workspace data.** Depending on your request, command inputs and results can include search terms, notebook and document identifiers, titles, note text, file names or contents, database records, and the data involved in a change. These may include personal data present in your prompts or notes. Data is used to answer your request and carry out the operation within the selected workspace.
- **Support communications.** If you contact SiYuan support, we receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond to your request. Share only details necessary for support. Public issue reports are visible to others.

## Who receives the data

The SiYuan command runs on your computer and returns its results to ChatGPT or Codex. OpenAI can process commands and results, including paths and note content, as part of your conversation. OpenAI handles this data under the policies and data controls that apply to your account or organization. The skill has no SiYuan-operated telemetry or note storage service. If you ask SiYuan to run a command that uses a configured external service, SiYuan can also send data to that service.

[OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) · [ChatGPT Data Controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-faq)

## Retention and choices

- The skill uses the selected path in the current conversation and keeps no separate connection profile or copy of your notes. Local command logs are handled by your SiYuan installation.
- Your workspace keeps its data according to your SiYuan storage, history, backup, and sync settings. To manage data already included in an OpenAI conversation, use the chat deletion and data controls that apply to your account or contact your organization administrator. OpenAI's retention period depends on the product, account, and settings.
- We retain private support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Public issue reports remain subject to the hosting service's retention and deletion controls.
- You can use ChatGPT or Codex controls to stop a conversation, restrict local execution, or remove the plugin. Select each workspace you want to use. Before asking the plugin to read or change content, consider what the conversation can receive and confirm that you have permission to share it.

For questions or privacy requests about the plugin, contact Yunnan Liandi Technology Co., Ltd. using the contact listed in the [SiYuan Privacy Policy](https://b3log.org/siyuan/en/privacy.html). For requests about data handled in your OpenAI account, use the [OpenAI Privacy Portal](https://privacy.openai.com/).
